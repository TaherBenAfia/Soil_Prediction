# 🌱 Soil Nutrient Gap Prediction for Sustainable Agriculture

## Problem

Soil health is fundamental to sustainable agriculture and directly impacts crop yield, food security, and long-term environmental stability. Essential macronutrients such as **Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K)**, along with secondary nutrients and micronutrients like **Calcium (Ca)** and **Magnesium (Mg)**, play a critical role in determining soil fertility and plant productivity.

However, traditional soil testing methods can be expensive, slow, and difficult to scale across large agricultural regions, especially in developing areas. Farmers often lack access to precise, data-driven recommendations for fertilizer application and soil management.

The challenge is to build a machine learning model capable of predicting the availability of **11 essential soil nutrients** and estimating nutrient gaps required for maize crops to reach a **target yield of 4 tons per hectare**, using soil and environmental data collected from farms across Africa.

---

## Solution

This project develops a machine learning pipeline that predicts soil nutrient availability and calculates nutrient gaps using historical soil measurements and environmental features.

By leveraging data-driven models, this solution enables scalable and cost-effective soil analysis. The predictions can help farmers:

- Optimize fertilizer usage  
- Improve soil management strategies  
- Reduce unnecessary agricultural costs  
- Increase crop productivity and sustainability  

The long-term vision is to support precision agriculture and contribute to smarter, data-driven farming practices.
